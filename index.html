Welcome to Pawfolio

Your pet's records are saved right here on this phone or browser — nothing is uploaded to the internet, so only you can see them.

To keep them safe, Pawfolio backs up your records automatically as you make changes — so if your phone breaks, you uninstall the app, or you clear your browser, you won't lose anything. Your backup stays on your device (or a file you choose); it's never uploaded.

You can also tap Export anytime to save a backup file yourself, or to move your records to a new phone.
