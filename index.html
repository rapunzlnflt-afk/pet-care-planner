Welcome to Pawfolio

Your pet's records are saved right here on this phone or browser — nothing is uploaded to the internet, so only you can see them.

Because of that, it's important to tap Export every week or two to save a backup. That way, if your phone breaks, you uninstall the app, or you clear your browser, you won't lose anything. You can also use the backup file to move your records to a new phone.

A friendly reminder will appear when it's time to back up.
